# Medora — Business Associate Agreement (Template)

**Status:** Template for hospital counsel review. Not a signed agreement until countersigned with your order form.

**Parties:** Covered Entity (Hospital) and Business Associate ({LEGAL_ENTITY placeholder — see order form}).

## 1. Purpose
This BAA permits Medora to create, receive, maintain, or transmit Protected Health Information (PHI) solely to provide Hospital Management System services.

## 2. Permitted uses and disclosures
- Provide, maintain, and support the Medora platform for the Hospital.
- De-identify PHI only where permitted by HIPAA and Hospital instructions.
- Disclose to subcontractors that agree to equivalent restrictions.

## 3. Safeguards
Business Associate shall implement administrative, physical, and technical safeguards consistent with the Security Rule, including access controls, encryption in transit, audit logging of PHI access, and workforce training.

## 4. Breach notification
Business Associate will notify Hospital without unreasonable delay and no later than **60 days** after discovery of a Breach of Unsecured PHI, including known affected individuals and mitigation steps.

## 5. Access, amendment, accounting
Business Associate will assist Hospital in fulfilling individual rights requests and produce relevant audit records of PHI access upon reasonable request.

## 6. Subcontractors
Current subprocessors are listed in `/legal/subprocessors.md`. Material changes will be notified per the DPA.

## 7. Term and termination
Upon termination, Business Associate will return or destroy PHI as directed, except where retention is required by law or backup impracticability (in which case PHI remains protected).
