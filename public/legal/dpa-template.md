# Medora — Data Processing Agreement (Template)

**Status:** Template for counsel. Final DPA issued with commercial order.

## 1. Roles
- **Controller / Covered Entity:** Hospital customer
- **Processor / Business Associate:** Medora operator

## 2. Subject matter
Processing of patient and staff data required to operate HMS modules (OPD, EMR specialty charts, lab, pharmacy, billing, hospital units, 3D anatomy markers).

## 3. Duration
For the subscription term plus retention required for legal / clinical record obligations.

## 4. Nature and purpose
Hosting, storage, backup, support, analytics (aggregated / de-identified where feasible), and optional AI features only when Hospital enables them under BAA flags.

## 5. Types of data
Identifiers, demographics, clinical notes, orders, results, billing metadata, staff accounts, audit logs.

## 6. Data subject categories
Patients, caregivers, hospital staff, contractors.

## 7. Subprocessors
See `subprocessors.md`. Hospital may object to material new subprocessors as set out in the signed DPA.

## 8. International transfers
Where applicable, Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent transfer mechanisms apply for transfers outside the Hospital’s jurisdiction.

## 9. Security
Encryption in transit (TLS), access control, PHI access audit export, backup, and vulnerability management as described on `/security`.

## 10. Audit rights
Hospital may request annual security questionnaire responses and evidence of PHI audit export capability.
